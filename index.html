Privacy Policy for Drop & Merge 2048

Last updated: 4 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Fenerich Soft ("we", "us", "our") handles information in connection with the mobile game Drop & Merge 2048 (the "Game"), Android package com.marcelfenerich.dropmerge2048, distributed through Google Play.

1. Summary

  • The Game has no accounts or login of our own. We do not ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
  • The Game offers optional sign-in through your Google Play Games profile, used only to submit leaderboard scores and unlock achievements. That profile belongs to your Google Account and is controlled by Google, not by us.
  • Your game progress (score, coins, power-ups) is stored only on your device.
  • The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played — which screens and features are used, and whether players return. If you are signed in to Play Games, your Play Games player identifier is used to recognise you across reinstalls.
  • The Game includes an advertising-mediation SDK but does not currently display any ads. Should that change, this policy will be updated first.
  • Purchases are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. We never receive or store your payment card or billing details.
  • The Game is not directed to children under 13.

2. Information collected

2.1 Information you provide

None directly to us. If you choose to sign in with Google Play Games, Google authenticates you and shares with the Game your Play Games player identifier, so that your run scores and achievement unlocks can be submitted to your Play Games profile. We do not receive your name, email address, or phone number, and this sign-in is entirely optional — the Game is fully playable without it.

That same player identifier is also sent to Google Analytics for Firebase as the analytics user identifier (see 2.4). This is what allows the Game's statistics to recognise a returning player rather than counting the same person again after a reinstall. It is a pseudonymous identifier issued by Google for this game; it is not your name or email address.

2.2 Information stored locally on your device

Game state — such as best score, coin balance, owned power-ups, saved run, and the identifiers of completed in-app purchases (used to prevent duplicate fulfilment) — is saved in local device storage. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the Game or clear its data.

2.3 Advertising SDKs (bundled, not currently active)

The Game bundles the Unity LevelPlay (ironSource) advertising-mediation SDK and the Unity Ads SDK. The Game does not currently display advertising, and these SDKs are not initialised, so they do not presently collect or share data. If advertising is introduced in a future version, those SDKs and the advertising networks they mediate may collect and share with third parties device and advertising identifiers (including the Google Advertising ID), technical device data such as device model, operating-system version, language and network type, and ad interaction data such as ads served, viewed and clicked. This policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration will be updated before that happens.

2.4 Analytics

The Game uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the Game is played. Data is processed by Google on our behalf and is not sold or shared with advertising partners. It collects:

  • Identifiers — an app-instance identifier generated by the SDK; the Google Advertising ID (GAID), which Firebase collects by default and which is used here for analytics rather than advertising; and, if you are signed in to Play Games, your Play Games player identifier, used as the analytics user identifier so that a returning player is not counted as a new one.
  • Automatically collected activity — app opens, sessions and time in the Game, app version, device model, operating-system version, language, and a coarse region derived from your IP address.
  • In-game events — runs started and finished and the statistics describing them (score, highest tile, duration and similar), power-ups used and bought, coin packs granted, and when the shop or coin store is opened. These describe gameplay, not you.
  • Purchase events — Google Play reports completed in-app purchases, including the amount and currency, to Firebase. We never receive your payment details.

We use this only in aggregate, to see which features are used and whether players return.

5. Legal bases (EEA / UK)

Where the EU or UK GDPR applies, we rely on: performance of a contract (providing the Game); and our legitimate interests in understanding how the Game is used, keeping it working, and improving it (analytics, in aggregate).

The Game does not serve personalised advertising and does not currently show an in-game consent prompt. If personalised advertising is introduced, consent will be collected before any such processing begins.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID — you can reset it or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads (wording varies by device). Deleting it limits ad personalisation.
  • Analytics — signing out of, or deleting, your Play Games profile stops your player identifier being used as the analytics identifier. Clearing the Game's data or uninstalling resets the app-instance identifier. To request deletion of analytics data already collected, contact us at the address in Section 10.
  • Local data — clear it any time via Android Settings → Apps → Drop & Merge 2048 → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling.
  • Play Games profile and account deletion — see Delete Account & Data for the steps.
  • GDPR / UK GDPR — rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection, and portability.
  • California (CCPA/CPRA) — rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of "sharing" of personal information for cross-context behavioural advertising.

To exercise any right, contact us at the address in Section 10. We hold no account or contact details for you, so please tell us whether you were signed in to Play Games — that is the only identifier by which analytics data can be located. We may also need to direct certain requests to the relevant third party.

7. Data retention

We operate no servers of our own and retain no personal data on them. Local game data persists on your device until you clear it or uninstall. Analytics data is held by Google Analytics for Firebase and retained for the period configured on our Analytics property, after which Google deletes the underlying user-level records; aggregated reporting may persist beyond that. Data held by other third-party SDKs is retained according to their own policies.

8. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

9. International transfers

Third-party providers may process data in countries other than yours, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.
