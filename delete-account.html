Delete Account & Data — Drop & Merge 2048

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Drop & Merge 2048 (developer: Fenerich Soft, Android package com.marcelfenerich.dropmerge2048) does not create or manage its own user accounts. When you sign in, that sign-in is your Google Play Games profile, which belongs to your Google Account and is controlled by Google, not by us. Your leaderboard scores and achievement unlocks for this game are stored as part of that Play Games profile.

How to delete your Play Games data

  1. Open the Google Play Games app on your Android device.
  2. Tap your profile picture in the top-right corner.
  3. Tap the settings (gear) icon → Play Games profile.
  4. Tap Delete Play Games profile and follow the on-screen steps.

This removes your Play Games identity, achievements, and leaderboard scores — for Drop & Merge 2048 and any other game using the same profile. It is managed entirely by Google; we do not receive or process the deletion request ourselves.

You can also review or delete data associated with your wider Google Account at myaccount.google.com/data-and-privacy.

What we ourselves store

Drop & Merge 2048 keeps no account data on any server we control. Your local game progress — best score, coin balance, owned power-ups, and saved run — lives only on your device. Delete it any time via Android Settings → Apps → Drop & Merge 2048 → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app.
